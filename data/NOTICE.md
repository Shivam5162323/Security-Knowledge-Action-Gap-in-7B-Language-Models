outputs. The retrieval corpus (`data/corpus.jsonl`, copied to `runs/paper/corpus.jsonl`)
is made of text from two public sources, which keep their own terms.

## MITRE CWE

The corpus contains the name, description and mitigation text of the entries of the
Common Weakness Enumeration, downloaded from https://cwe.mitre.org.
